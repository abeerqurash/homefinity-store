store policies

What to expect when you order from homefinity.pk, in plain words. Questions? Contact us.

Shipping & deliveryPaymentReturns & warrantyPrivacyTerms of useCookies & storage

Shipping & delivery

Delivery fee

The delivery fee is Rs. 200, and delivery is free on orders over Rs. 3,000. Your cart shows how close you are to free delivery, and the exact amount is shown again at checkout.

Delivery times

Delivery times depend on your city and the courier. We will confirm an estimate with you when your order is confirmed. We do not promise a delivery window before the order is confirmed.

Tracking

Every order gets a tracking code. Paste it on the Track order page to see the latest status.

Payment

Cash on delivery

Payment is cash on delivery. You pay the courier when your order arrives, so no card or bank details are needed on this website.

Prices

All prices are in Pakistani rupees (Rs.). Prices and stock are checked on our server when you place an order, so the total you confirm is the total you pay.

Returns & warranty

Returns and refunds

Our return and refund terms are being finalised. If something is wrong with your order, contact us as soon as it arrives and we will sort it out with you.

Warranty

Warranty terms depend on the product and will be shown on the product page where they apply. If a product page does not mention a warranty, none is promised.

Privacy

What we collect

When you order we ask for your name, phone number, city and delivery address. We use them to deliver your order and contact you about it, and for nothing else.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for card numbers. You do not need an account to order.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your order details at any time through the Contact page.

Terms of use

Orders

An order is confirmed once we have checked stock and your details. We may contact you by phone to confirm it. If an item is out of stock we will tell you before dispatching.

Product information

We describe products as accurately as we can. Photos are for illustration, and colours can look slightly different on different screens.

Cookies & storage

What the site stores

The site uses your browser storage to remember what is in your cart. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies at the moment.

Still have a question?

Send us a message and we will get back to you.
